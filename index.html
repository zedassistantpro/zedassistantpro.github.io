ZED Bronze Assistant

A Chrome side panel for MSME ZED Bronze assessors · Free and Pro

It is a typing assistant for the part of a ZED Bronze assessment that is typing. It asks what you can see in the evidence in front of you, and drafts the remark from your answers. It does not assess anything, and it is not part of the ZED programme.

What it does

You open a requirement on the ZED portal as you normally would. The panel recognises which one you opened and shows the evidence checklist for it: the handful of things that requirement actually turns on. You answer them from what is in front of you, and the remark assembles itself as you go — what was provided first, then anything missing or queried, each point numbered.

  • The wording follows your answers. Change an answer, the remark changes.
  • Every non-conformity point is numbered, so nothing reads as a single run-on objection.
  • It is a draft of the typing, not a verdict. Read it before you submit it.
  • A History tab keeps a short diagnostics log with a Copy for support button, holding no remark text, no unit details and no assessment id.

Who it is for

Assessors and assessment agencies carrying out ZED Bronze certification assessments for MSMEs on the ZED portal. If you have never written a ZED remark, this tool has nothing to offer you — it assumes you know what the requirement means and are simply tired of typing the same careful sentences.

What it does not do

It does not decide whether evidence is acceptable, and it makes no claim about how an assessment will turn out. The verdict is always yours. It also depends on the ZED portal staying much as it is, so when the portal is updated parts of it can stop working until a matching update is released. The full statement is here — it is worth two minutes before you rely on it.

Privacy, in one paragraph

No assessment information is ever sent anywhere: not the unit, not the remarks, not the evidence, not the verdicts. The free build sends nothing at all. Pro sends only its licence key and the assessor ID shown as signed in, about twice a day, to check the licence is valid. Your portal password is never read, stored or transmitted. Questions people ask

Is this official?

No. It is an independent tool, not made, endorsed, reviewed or supported by the Quality Council of India, the Ministry of MSME, or anyone who runs the ZED scheme or its portal.

Does it submit the assessment for me?

No. Free never presses Submit. Pro fills and submits only the individual requirements you explicitly tick. The final submission of an assessment is never touched by either.

How do I get a Pro licence?

Send a licence request through the form, with the ZED assessor ID it should be issued against. A key is issued against that one ID and runs a month at a time.

Which browsers?

Chromium browsers with Manifest V3 side panel support — Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge among them. There is no Firefox or Safari version.

Something is wrong with the wording it suggested.

Tell us which requirement and what it should have said. Wording bugs are worth fixing for everyone, and that is the most useful message you can send.
