Privacy policy

ZED Bronze Assistant Free and Pro · last updated 21 October 2026

The short version: your assessment work never leaves your machine, and the free build never sends anything at all.

What stays in your browser

The assistant runs inside your own browser. Your work does not leave your machine. The remark is composed in your browser from the answers you give. The extension keeps, in your own browser only:

  • which side of the window you want the panel on;
  • the requirements you ticked for auto-fill (Pro);
  • a history of the remarks you filled, and values you asked it to remember, in the browser's local database;
  • your licence key (Pro).

None of that is transmitted to us, and we cannot read it.

What is sent, and only in Pro

Pro checks that its licence is valid, roughly twice a day. That request contains the licence key and the ZED assessor ID the portal shows as signed in on that computer, and goes to a licence service we operate. It returns only whether the key is valid and when it expires.

No assessment information is sent: not the unit, not the remarks, not the evidence, not the verdicts.

When you ask for a licence we hold the name, email, ZED assessor ID and phone number you send us, together with the licence dates. We use them to issue your licence, to check it, and to contact you about renewal. Nothing else.

Who else handles it

The licence service and the records behind it run on Google Workspace, so Google processes that information on our behalf and under their own terms. The extension itself uses no other third party, and carries no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

This website

These pages — not the extension — count visits, using Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless: it stores nothing on your device, gives you no identifier, and cannot recognise you on a later visit or follow you to any other site. It records which page was opened, which site linked to it, the country, and the browser and device type. That is all, and none of it identifies you.

We use it to see whether assessors are finding these pages at all. The extension never contacts it, and the page shown after you uninstall is not counted.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or transfer your information to anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising, scoring, lending or any purpose unrelated to running the licence.
  • We do not read, store or transmit your portal password. The extension reads only the user ID field on the login page, to know which assessor is signed in, and never the password or the captcha.
  • We do not take payments inside the extension and never ask for card or bank details in it.

Keeping and deleting

Licence records are kept while your licence is active and for one year after, so a renewal can be matched up. Ask us and we will delete your record; your licence stops working when we do.

Everything held in your browser is removed when you uninstall the extension, or from the extension's own History tab.

Contact

Ask us anything about this policy, or about the record we hold for you: zedassistantpro@gmail.com, or the support form.
