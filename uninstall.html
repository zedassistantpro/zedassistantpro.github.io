The requirement I needed wasn't covered

That is the most common reason, and it is worth knowing why. The free assistant deliberately covers two sections in full — Site Exterior Photos and Product Photographs & Description — rather than all seventeen badly.

The other fifteen requirements, the final submission wording, the location checks and auto-fill are in Pro, which is a separate extension. If you met the locked card at requirement 1.1 and assumed the whole thing was a tease, that is our fault for not saying so sooner.

It didn't work on the portal

This is the one we most want to hear about. The assistant works alongside the ZED portal, so when the portal is updated parts of it can stop working until a matching update is released - and Chrome reviews every update, so that takes a few days. We do not always find out straight away. If the extension is still installed somewhere, the History tab has a short diagnostics log and a Copy for support button — it contains no remark text, no unit details and no assessment id.

Send that, with a line about what you were doing, and we will tell you whether it is fixed.
