What the assistant does, and what it does not

ZED Bronze Assistant Free and Pro · last updated 19 October 2026

It is a typing assistant for the part of a ZED assessment that is typing. It does not assess anything, and it is not part of the ZED programme.

It is not affiliated with the ZED portal

This is an independent tool. It is not made, endorsed, reviewed or supported by the Quality Council of India, the Ministry of MSME, or anyone who runs the ZED scheme or its portal. Nothing it suggests carries any official standing.

It depends on the ZED portal

The assistant works alongside the portal, so it depends on the portal staying much as it is.

When the portal is updated, parts of the assistant can stop working until a matching update is released. Chrome reviews every extension update, so that usually takes a few days. The portal is maintained independently of us, so a change can arrive without notice and we cannot rule this out.

Two things limit what it costs you. The panel says so when it can no longer do its job, rather than leaving you to guess. And your work in the portal is unaffected — remarks can be written there exactly as they would be without the assistant — so an interruption costs time, never an assessment.

If you do run into it, the History tab holds a short diagnostics log with a Copy for support button. Sending it helps us put things right sooner.

The verdict is always yours

The assistant suggests wording from the answers you give it. It does not decide whether evidence is acceptable, and it makes no claim about how an assessment will turn out. Read what it writes before you submit it — it is a draft of the typing, not a judgement.

The free assistant never presses Submit. Pro can fill and submit the requirements you explicitly tick, and marks every one of them in red on the page so you can see which were written without you watching. The final submission of an assessment is never touched by either.

Licensing, for Pro

A Pro licence is issued to one assessor and checked about twice a day. If that check cannot be made — no signal in a unit, for instance — Pro keeps working for a week on its last answer rather than locking you out mid-assessment.
